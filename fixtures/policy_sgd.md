# Expense Reimbursement Policy, Singapore (written for testing)

> This policy was written for the Receipted project as a test fixture. It imitates the rules of a student
> club or small business in Singapore so the checker has something realistic to enforce. Amounts are in
> Singapore dollars (SGD). It is not a real organisation's policy.

### C1. Purpose of expense
Expenses are reimbursable only when incurred for an approved club activity or business purpose. The claim must state the purpose. Claims with no stated purpose are held for review.

### C2. Original itemised receipt required
Every claim must be supported by an original receipt that lists each item purchased with its price. A receipt that shows only a grand total, or a card slip on its own, is not sufficient and the claim is held for review until an itemised receipt is provided.

### C3. Alcohol and tobacco
Alcoholic drinks and tobacco products are never reimbursable. This includes beer (Tiger, Heineken, Asahi, craft beer), wine, spirits, soju, sake, makgeolli, cocktails, and cigarettes or vapes. A claim that contains any such item is rejected for that item.

### C4. Claimed amount must match the receipt
The amount claimed must equal the total printed on the receipt. A claim for more than the receipt total is rejected. A claim for less than the receipt total is acceptable (the claimant may choose not to claim some items).

### C5. Agreed price list
For items that appear on the agreed price list, the unit price paid must not exceed the agreed maximum unit price for that item. Any amount above the agreed maximum is not reimbursed and the claim is held for review.

### C6. Personal and non-business items
Personal items are not reimbursable: toiletries, skincare and cosmetics, clothing, phone top-ups, gift cards, games, lottery, medicines and medical or dental treatment for personal use, and gifts for individuals. Such items are rejected.

### C7. Two approvers above SGD 500
Any single claim above SGD 500 requires sign-off from two approvers before payment. The checker must mark such claims for review rather than approving them automatically.

### C8. GST and service charge
GST and a restaurant service charge printed on the receipt are reimbursable as printed. Voluntary tips and gratuities added by the claimant are not reimbursable.

### C9. Quantities
The quantity of any item claimed must not exceed the quantity shown on the receipt, and the same receipt may not be claimed twice.

### C10. Unreadable or damaged receipts
If the receipt is unreadable, torn, faded or partly missing so that items or totals cannot be confirmed, the claim is held for manual review. The checker must not guess missing values.

### C11. Submission deadline
Claims must be submitted within 30 days of the purchase date on the receipt. Late claims are held for review by the treasurer. (The automated checker cannot yet verify dates and leaves this to the reviewer.)

### C12. Meals and hospitality
Meals are reimbursable when they are part of an approved activity. Meal claims above SGD 40 per person are held for review. The number of people must be stated for any meal claim above SGD 40 in total.

### C13. Packaging and bag fees
Plastic bag, packaging and takeaway charges are reimbursable up to a total of SGD 1 per receipt. Amounts above this are not reimbursed.
